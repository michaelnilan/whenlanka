_Last updated: November 2026_

## Analytics

We use [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io), which is cookie-free and doesn't collect personal data or track you across sites. We record page views and a few anonymous events, such as clicks on partner links and form submissions, to learn which routes are useful.

## The driver quote form

If you request a driver quote, the details you enter (name, email, dates, group size and your route) are sent through [Formspree](https://formspree.io) to our inbox. We forward them to one local driver partner so they can quote you, and keep them only as long as needed to handle your request. Ask us to delete them at any time by replying to any email from us.

## Affiliate links

Links marked as partner links (Booking.com, Agoda, GetYourGuide, 12Go, BookRetreats) take you to those sites, which may set their own cookies so they can credit us with a commission. Their privacy policies apply once you're on their site.

## Maps

Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap, which receives your IP address as part of serving them. Fonts are hosted on this site.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Use the form on any itinerary page and mention "privacy".
