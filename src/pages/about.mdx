**whensrilanka** answers one question: *"I can travel in month X for N days. Where in Sri Lanka should I go?"*

Sri Lanka is small, but two monsoons hit opposite coasts at opposite times of year, so the best route in January looks nothing like the best route in July. Most itineraries online ignore this. Ours start from it.

## How routes are made

Every route comes from the same small open dataset: six climate regions scored month by month, about 35 stops, and realistic travel times between them. A deterministic engine filters out regions in monsoon, ranks the remaining stops by how iconic they are and how well they match your interests, fits them into your nights, and orders them into the shortest sensible loop from the airport. It prefers the scenic hill-country train. The same engine runs on every page and in the live planner, so they always agree.

## How we make money

We never take bookings or payments. We earn a commission if you book a stay, tour or train ticket through a marked link, and we pass private-driver requests to a small number of local operators. Monastery and hermitage pages carry no commercial links, apart from clearly marked lay meditation retreats.

## Corrections

Climate figures are approximate averages, and practical details change. If you spot something wrong, especially on the [Sacred & Silent](/sacred) pages, where accuracy matters most, please tell us using the driver form on any itinerary page with "Correction" in the route box. We read every message.
