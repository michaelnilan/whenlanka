French travellers often combine Sri Lanka's heritage sites with a long beach stay. The **February winter holidays** are close to perfect timing: the whole south, west and centre of the island are dry.

## Christmas and February holidays

<RouteSummary days={14} month={2} />

Add a few days at a south-coast beach (Tangalle is the quietest, Mirissa the liveliest) or extend into the hill country with a stay on a tea estate.

## Spring holidays (April)

Hot inter-monsoon weather with afternoon storms. Sinhala and Tamil New Year in mid-April is a festive time to be there, but transport is busy.

## July–August

The southwest monsoon is in full swing. Go east: Trincomalee and Pasikuda have calm, swimmable seas, and Arugam Bay is in surf season. The Cultural Triangle is dry and the Minneriya elephant "Gathering" peaks in August.

## Good to know

- **Language:** English is widely spoken in tourism; French much less so outside larger hotels.
- **Time difference:** +4 h 30 (winter) / +3 h 30 (summer).
