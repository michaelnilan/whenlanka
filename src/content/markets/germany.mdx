German travellers tend to take Sri Lanka slowly, with two or three weeks, a rail journey through the tea country and time for Ayurveda. Those longer trips let you add a second coast or the Tamil north.

## Winter (December–March)

The south and west coasts, the hill country and the Cultural Triangle are all in season. With three weeks you can add Ayurveda days around Bentota or the quiet beaches of Tangalle.

## Summer holidays

Your Sommerferien fall in the southwest monsoon. Go **east and north**: Trincomalee, Pasikuda and Arugam Bay are at their sunniest, the Cultural Triangle is dry, and Jaffna is an easy addition on a three-week trip.

## Autumn holidays (Herbstferien)

October is the wettest month for much of the island. If those are your dates, keep the beach part short and spend more time in Kandy and the Cultural Triangle, where showers usually come in the afternoon.

## Good to know

- **Ayurveda:** authentic Ayurveda resorts usually want a minimum stay of 7–14 days. Plan it as a separate block at the start or end.
- **Time difference:** +4 h 30 (winter) / +3 h 30 (summer).
