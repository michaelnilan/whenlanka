The UK's winter is Sri Lanka's best season on the south and west coasts, which makes it one of the most reliable long-haul winter-sun trips. But British school holidays fall across both monsoons, so the coast you pick matters more than the dates.

## Christmas, New Year and February half-term

This is classic Sri Lanka: Sigiriya, Kandy, the train to Ella, a leopard safari at Yala and a beach finish at Mirissa or Galle. Seas on the south coast are calm and blue whales pass close to shore.

## Easter

April is the inter-monsoon month: hot and humid, with afternoon thunderstorms, but generally workable everywhere. It's also Sinhala and Tamil New Year (mid-April), when many locals travel and some businesses close for a few days.

## Summer holidays (late July–August)

The southwest monsoon means the south coast is rough. **Flip the trip east**: Trincomalee's calm bays, the Arugam Bay surf season and the Cultural Triangle at its driest. August also brings "The Gathering" of elephants at Minneriya.

## Good to know

- **Time difference:** +5 h 30 (+4 h 30 during British Summer Time).
- **Driving:** UK licences aren't valid on their own. Most visitors hire a car with a driver, which is affordable and far less stressful.
- **Plugs:** a mix of UK-style (Type G) and older round-pin sockets. Bring a universal adapter.
