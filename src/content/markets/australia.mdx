Sri Lanka is one of the closest "big trip" destinations from Australia, and there's a large Sri Lankan community, so flights are frequent. Australia's main holiday windows happen to line up well with Sri Lanka's two dry seasons.

## Summer holidays (December–January)

The south and west coasts and the hill country are in season. Do the classic route: Sigiriya, Kandy, the train to Ella, a Yala safari and the south coast.

## July and September school holidays

The east coast is at its best: surf at Arugam Bay, snorkelling at Pigeon Island off Trincomalee, and calm, clear water. Combine it with the Cultural Triangle and Ella, which is drier than the western hills at this time.

## Good to know

- **Time difference:** Sri Lanka is 4 h 30 behind Sydney (5 h 30 during daylight saving).
- **Surf season:** Arugam Bay runs from roughly April to October; the south coast (Weligama, Hikkaduwa) from November to April.
