Russian travellers make up one of Sri Lanka's largest visitor groups in winter, mostly heading for the south-west coast. The beach is easy, but the island rewards anyone who builds in a few days of culture and tea country.

## New Year and January holidays

The south and west coasts are at their best, and the hill country is clear and cool. A 10-day trip can combine Sigiriya and Kandy with a week on the coast.

## May holidays

The southwest monsoon arrives in May, so the classic beaches turn rough. Head for the east coast (Pasikuda, Trincomalee) and the Cultural Triangle instead.

## Good to know

- **Payments:** check before you travel whether your cards work abroad; bring enough foreign cash (US dollars or euros) to exchange at banks or licensed exchangers.
- **Time difference:** +2 h 30 from Moscow.
